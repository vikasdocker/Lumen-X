## Intake Rules

- Prefer CC0, public domain, Apache-2.0, MIT, or similarly permissive assets.
- Treat free-asset marketplace listings, including OpenGameArt.org listings, as unverified until the exact license text and creator attribution have been checked.
- Record the original source URL, creator, license name, attribution requirement, commercial-use status, and modification status.
- Store source license text, screenshots, or exported license pages in `assets/licenses/` when practical.
- Do not import an asset when the license is unclear, missing, non-commercial only, or incompatible with commercial distribution.
- Keep generated or procedural in-repo visuals documented as internal work, but do not list them as external assets.
